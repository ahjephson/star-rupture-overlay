Open the Codex in Update 3

Press K during gameplay. Search Production, Buildings and Interiors by name. Browse Equipment and Corporations without a search filter. Your search text returns when you switch back to a searchable tab. Select linked ingredients, buildings or unlocks to open their details. For example, select Meteor Heart from Base Core to open its Production entry. Use Esc, Tab or the close button to return to gameplay.

Use Pin recipe or Pin upgrade to keep materials in view. Locked recipes show unlock requirements and progress. Unlocked production recipes show their inputs. The Base Core entry shows the material requirements for unlocked levels and the next unlock.

Use Back or Forward to retrace links you followed from the detail pane. The controls also work with Alt+Left, Alt+Right and mouse buttons assigned to browser Back and Forward.

See all five tabs in game.

Rupture countdown

The timer shows STABLE, PRE-WAVE, APPROACHING, INCINERATING, COOLING or GROWBACK with the time remaining. It follows game time and pauses when the game does. In 1.2.0, choose which stages show the panel under Settings > Wave timer > Visible stages. The overlay keeps tracking the wave while the panel is hidden. When the stage is known but its timing is unavailable, the timer shows --:--.

To move the timer, choose Unlock to move in the tray menu, drag it, then choose Lock position. Reset position restores automatic placement. The display moves below the pause-menu border.

Pin a recipe in Update 2

Keep the game focused, open the recipe station, hover over an unfinished recipe row and press Alt+X. The pinned recipe stays visible when you leave the station.

  • Hover over a different unfinished recipe and press the shortcut to switch to it.
  • Use the shortcut on the same recipe to unpin it.
  • Use the shortcut outside the station to clear the pin.
  • The panel closes when you unlock the recipe.

Pin recipes and upgrades in Update 3

In the Codex, select Pin recipe to track production inputs or unlock requirements. Select Pin upgrade to track machine or Base Core upgrade materials. You can keep up to three pins across the Codex and Recipe Station, including corporation deliveries.

At the Recipe Station, hover over an unfinished recipe and press Alt+X, or double-click its icon or name. You can turn off double-click pinning in Settings.

  • Use the shortcut or double-click on a pinned recipe to remove it.
  • The shortcut outside the station clears all pins. Double-clicking outside the station does not.
  • Remove one panel with its × button while the game's mouse cursor is visible.
  • Recipe Station pins close automatically when you unlock the recipe. Your pins restore after restarting the overlay and when switching between local worlds.

Read the pinned panels

Unlocked production recipes show their input icons. Hover over an ingredient while the game cursor is visible to see its name.

Recipe Station unlock pins show submitted and required amounts. A tick marks a completed requirement. Red counters mean you have none of that material in your bag; white counters mean you have some. The smaller inset number shows how many you have in your bag.

In Update 3, the bag quantity turns green when it covers the remaining requirement, or orange when it covers only some. Use the game's INSERT buttons at the Recipe Station to submit unlock materials.

Pin corporation deliveries

  1. Open the Codex and select a corporation on the Corporations tab. The detail pane opens at its next level and shows your reputation progress.
  2. Under Delivery options, check how much reputation each item earns and how many of that item would reach the next level. The amounts are alternatives, so you can deliver a mix. A padlock marks an item you haven’t unlocked.
  3. Select Pin deliveries to keep those quantities on the HUD. The amounts update as you earn reputation, and the pin clears when you reach the target.

Follow a Personal Marker in Update 3

Set a Personal Marker in the game. The floating compass follows your viewing direction, showing the marker's bearing and distance. A side arrow points toward a marker outside the visible compass range. You do not create markers in the overlay.

The compass measures straight-line distance on the horizontal plane. It does not account for elevation or show a walking route.

The arrival cue

By default, the marker turns cyan and shows ARRIVED within 25 metres. The cue clears beyond 35 metres, or when you move or remove the marker. Set both distances or turn the cue off in Settings > Compass. The clearing distance must be greater than the arrival distance so the cue does not flicker at the boundary.

The compass hides during pause, while the game's mouse cursor is visible, when the game loses focus or when current navigation readings are unavailable. It still works as a compass without a marker.
