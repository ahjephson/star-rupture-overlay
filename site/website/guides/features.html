The Update 3 pre-release is available for the Public Test Branch (PTB). Screenshots on the product page show Update 3 features.

Rupture countdown

The timer shows STABLE, PRE-WAVE, APPROACHING, INCINERATING, COOLING or GROWBACK with the time remaining. It follows game time and pauses when the game does. If a current reading is unavailable, it shows --:--.

To move the timer, choose Unlock to move in the tray menu, drag it, then choose Lock position. Reset position restores automatic placement. The display moves below the pause-menu border.

Pin a recipe in Update 2

Keep the game focused, open the recipe station, hover over an unfinished recipe row and press Alt+X. The pinned recipe stays visible when you leave the station.

  • Hover over a different unfinished recipe and press the shortcut to switch to it.
  • Use the shortcut on the same recipe to unpin it.
  • Use the shortcut outside the station to clear the pin.
  • The panel closes when the recipe's research is complete.

Pin recipes in Update 3

Hover over an unfinished recipe row and use Alt+X, or double-click the recipe's icon or name at the station. You can keep up to three recipes pinned. You can turn double-click pinning off in Settings.

  • Use the shortcut or double-click on a pinned recipe to remove it.
  • The shortcut outside the station clears all pins. Double-clicking outside the station does not.
  • Remove one panel with its × button while the game's mouse cursor is visible.
  • Completed recipes close automatically. Your pins restore after restarting the overlay and when switching between local worlds.

Read the recipe panels

Each ingredient shows submitted and required amounts. A tick marks a completed requirement. Red counters mean you have none of that material in your bag; white counters mean you have some. The smaller inset number shows how many you have in your bag.

In Update 3, the bag quantity turns green when it covers the remaining requirement, or orange when it covers only some. Hover over an ingredient while the game's mouse cursor is visible to see its name. Submit materials using the game's INSERT buttons.

Follow a Personal Marker in Update 3

Set a Personal Marker in the game. The floating compass follows your viewing direction, showing the marker's bearing and distance. A side arrow points toward a marker outside the visible compass range. You do not create markers in the overlay.

The compass measures straight-line distance on the horizontal plane. It does not account for elevation or show a walking route.

The arrival cue

By default, the marker turns cyan and shows ARRIVED within 25 metres. The cue clears beyond 35 metres, or when you move or remove the marker. Set both distances or turn the cue off in Settings > Compass. The clearing distance must be greater than the arrival distance so the cue does not flicker at the boundary.

The compass hides during pause, while the game's mouse cursor is visible, when the game loses focus or when current navigation readings are unavailable. It still works as a compass without a marker.
